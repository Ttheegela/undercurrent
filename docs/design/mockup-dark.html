What the records show

The United States listed TASSOS on 30 July 2025 under its Iran sanctions, blocking it as property in which its manager, Zulu Ships Management & Operation, has an interest.12 The European Union followed on 24 October 2025 for carrying Russian crude oil or oil products,3 and the United Kingdom on 24 February 2026.4

Before that, the Norwegian-run tanker company Frontline sold the ship in February 2023 to a Liberian-registered company whose name is not public.5
