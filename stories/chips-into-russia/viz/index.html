How chips reach Russia: the routes in the trade records

Electronic chips (customs code HS 8542) recorded arriving in Russia, grouped by the country they were shipped from. In these records the middle countries only start appearing after the invasion; the chips themselves were mostly made elsewhere.

How to read this. Every number is a count of shipment records in Sayari's trade data, not a value, a volume, or proof that any rule was broken. Each row is a lead, not a finding: it points to where a person should look next.
