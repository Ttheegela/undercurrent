*Trace the Unseen build day, Fri 25 Sept 2026, Sanctions & evasion track. Written for teammates. Updated 15:08.*
*Documented conduct is stated plainly with its source; our own inferences are called **leads**. People are named only when an official list or government release names them.*

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A sanctions list only works when someone recognises the name. After Russia's full invasion of Ukraine (24 Feb 2022), Western chips kept reaching Russia through **new countries** (Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, the UAE, Armenia, Turkey) and **short-lived companies**. Governments did list those companies, but **15 to 20 months** after they first appeared, and most had already moved on. What stays the same for years is **the product, the buyer and the route**. So our demo, for a chip distributor's compliance officer, says: *names change in weeks; the need doesn't.*

## 5. Findings beyond the demo (verified, good places to expand)

- **Turkey as a hub.** 21,537 chip shipments from Turkey into Russia in the records; fewer than 5% Turkish-made. One logistics firm (BRK Uluslararasi Nakliyat, OFAC 30 Oct 2024) carries about 45%; one buyer (Ural Telekom Sistems, EU-restricted) receives 61%. 9 of the top 10 shipper firms are now on US lists, listed 2 to 31 months after they started.
- **RM Design's Turkey leg.** 76 records under the Kyrgyz firm's own name leaving Turkey (Aug 2022 to Sep 2023), 40 of them after its US listing, most giving an Istanbul address. It left the Kyrgyz route 24 days before being listed.
- **Firms run several countries at once.** Rama Group and Shisan shipped from Kyrgyzstan, Thailand and Hong Kong in parallel and dropped the Kyrgyz leg before any listing; both shared listed buyers (Katrade, Sol Group, OFAC 23 Aug 2024).
- **Screening gaps we proved.** Sayari called ITIC clean; OFAC listed it on 12 Jun 2024. A Russian firm in the sponsor data shares tax number, registration number and address with UK-listed LLC Phoenix but is flagged clean. Our own checker still misses GTME (listed as "ZAO GTME Tekhnologii").
- **The buyer persists.** Testkomplekt appears as one record from 2019 to 2025; its Western suppliers stopped by 2 Mar 2022; six Asian suppliers replaced them, all later US-listed; it kept receiving 22 months after its own listing.
- **The part persists.** Xilinx chips reached Russia under 58 shipper names, 45 of them seen in a single month.
- **AI chips.** 2,474 NVIDIA-labelled records into Russia 2019 to 2026; China is nearly dark in this data. Shreya Life Sciences was reported shipping H100 servers to Russia and was OFAC-listed two days later (30 Oct 2024).
- **Medicine and humanitarian goods** (the other side of sanctions): see `sanctions-explained/` for exemptions and over-compliance.

## 7. Rules we keep

- State documented conduct plainly with its source: government findings, and what the records directly show (for example "kept shipping after being sanctioned"). Describe conduct; don't write "illegal" unless a court or agency said so.
- Use "lead" only for our own inferences about firms that aren't listed (timing, a shared address, a one-ship company).
- Listing dates from **official pages only**, and the **first** restriction counts (for ELEM Group that is the US Commerce Entity List, 7 Dec 2023).
- **Two independent signals** before calling a pattern, and **ID, address and country agreement** before saying two records are the same company.
- Every number traces to a saved raw answer; our verifier agent re-derives every figure.
- People are named only when an official list or government release names them, in that role.
- "Leaving Turkey" is the **declared** country, not a traced route. Shipment **records**, not dollar values.
