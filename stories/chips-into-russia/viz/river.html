Same river, new boats

Names change in weeks. The flow, the buyer and the part stay for years.

Computer chips (product code HS 8542) kept reaching Russia through Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan while the companies carrying them came and went. By the time an official list named a company, the flow had usually moved to another one. The Russian buyer and the need stayed the same.

How to read this. Each coloured band is one shipping company; band height is the number of shipment records per month. Black pins are official listings (solid = checked on the official page; dashed = approximate date from a public sanctions dataset). The hatched grey zone is where Russia's customs data stops, not necessarily the trade. Hover or tap anything to see its source file. Every item here is a lead, not a finding. Listing bodies: US Treasury's sanctions office (OFAC), US Commerce export blacklist (BIS Entity List), UK and EU sanctions lists.
