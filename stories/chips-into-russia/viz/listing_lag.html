How long before the lists caught up

For each company in our checked findings: when it first looked suspicious in the records (registered just after the invasion, or first seen trading on a new route), when an official sanctions or export list named it, and whether shipments were still recorded after that.

How to read this. Grey dots are shipment records before the company was listed; blue dots are records after. The shaded blue zone is "after listing". Solid vertical lines are official listings, with the list and date. Each row is a lead, not a finding: a record is not proof that any rule was broken. Tap or hover any dot or label to see its source.
